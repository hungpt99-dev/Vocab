## Detailed description

```
Save words while you browse. Understand and remember them.

AI Vocabulary Saver turns the words you meet while reading into a vocabulary
you actually keep: one click saves any word with its sentence, it is
highlighted everywhere it appears again, and an AI explanation builds the
meaning, translation, examples and synonyms for you.

WHAT IT DOES

• Save as you read — select a word on any page and save it from the floating
  card, the right-click menu, or the Ctrl+Shift+S shortcut. The sentence it
  appeared in is captured with it.

• Highlights everywhere — saved words are marked on every page you read
  afterward. Hover a highlight to see its meaning, note and when you saved it.

• Bilingual reading — turn a page into a bilingual book (Alt+Shift+R):
  word-by-word glosses and sentence translations appear under the original
  text as you read. Works out of the box with no AI key.

• AI explanations with YOUR key — the extension never asks for payment or an
  account. Bring your own API key for OpenAI (or OpenAI-compatible providers,
  Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Ollama, LM Studio) and explain any
  saved word: meaning, simple explanation, translation, examples, synonyms,
  antonyms, pronunciation, collocations and etymology — everything in one card.

• A review system built in — the words you save are scheduled for review with
  spaced repetition, so they move from "met once" to "yours".

• Local-first — your vocabulary, notes and tags live in your browser profile.
  Export and import keep them portable. Nothing is uploaded, ever.

PRIVACY

• No account, no sign-in, no telemetry, no analytics, no ads.
• Your vocabulary never leaves your device.
• Your AI key stays in your browser and is sent only to the provider you
  configured.
• Reading features use Google's public keyless Translate endpoint — no key
  needed, no data stored.
• See the developer's privacy policy for the full data-use disclosure.

Works on any website. Free forever.
```
