## Full Description

**Vocab helps you discover, understand, save, and learn vocabulary while browsing the web.**

### Save vocabulary effortlessly
Select any word or phrase on a page and save it with the floating toolbar, the right-click menu, or the keyboard shortcut (Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+S). Vocab stores the word, the sentence it appeared in, and the source page so your list is always grounded in real context.

### Understand words with AI — using your own key
Vocab explains saved words with an AI provider you configure yourself (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Mistral, Groq, Together, or a local model via Ollama/LM Studio). You bring your own API key (or run a local model with no key at all), so your requests go directly from your browser to the provider you choose. Vocab never sells or shares your data, and there is no Vocab-owned AI backend in the middle.

### See your words highlighted as you read
Vocab highlights your saved vocabulary directly on the web pages you visit, so the words you're learning keep surfacing in context. Highlighting works whether the reading mode is Off, Allowed sites, or Everywhere — it follows your vocabulary, not a site allow-list.

### Vocab Radar
Vocab Radar surfaces related vocabulary worth learning next. When you explain a saved word, Vocab generates a set of connected terms (synonyms, collocations, related phrases) and highlights them on pages too — expanding your vocabulary organically from the words you already care about.

### Review and learn
The Library tab in the popup is your personal dictionary: every saved word with its meaning, translation, examples, and notes. Track what you've learned, star favorites, and revisit words anytime.

### Privacy-first
- Your vocabulary, notes, and settings are stored **locally** in your browser (Chrome storage + IndexedDB).
- AI requests are sent **only** to the provider you configure, using **your** API key (or a local model). Translation can optionally use a keyless Google Translate endpoint.
- No accounts, no telemetry, no advertising, no data resale.
- An AI API key is **optional** — local models work with no key; without any provider configured, saving and highlighting still work, only the AI explanations are unavailable.

### Who it's for
Students, language learners, researchers, and avid readers who want to turn everyday reading into a growing, personal vocabulary — without handing their data to yet another service.
