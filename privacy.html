Vocab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026
This policy applies to the Vocab Chrome extension. It describes exactly what the extension processes, what stays on your device, and what leaves it. It was written against the implementation itself — nothing here is aspirational.

The short version: Vocab is a local-first vocabulary learning extension. No account, no sign-in, no telemetry, no analytics, no ads, and no backend. Your vocabulary and settings never leave your browser. Two things can leave your device, and only when a feature you use requires them: text you ask to be explained is sent to the AI provider you configured, and text from the page you are reading may be sent to Google's keyless Translate endpoint for the bilingual-reading feature.

1. What data the extension processes

When you use Vocab, it processes the following data. All of it exists only because the vocabulary-learning features need it.

Data When it is processed Stored? Sent anywhere?
Words you select or save on a webpage When you save, explain or translate a selection Yes — locally Only to your AI provider or Google Translate when you trigger it
The sentence a saved word appeared in, plus up to 200 characters of preceding text When you save or explain a word Yes — locally Only to your AI provider when you trigger an explanation
Page title and URL of the page you saved a word from When you save a word Yes — locally Page title/URL may be included in an AI explanation prompt you trigger
Article text of the page you are reading Only while the bilingual-reading feature is active on that page Session cache only To Google's keyless Translate endpoint (see §3)

Browsing history Never No Never
Passwords, form input, payment information Never No Never

2. Where data is stored

Everything Vocab stores lives inside your browser profile:

  • Your vocabulary (saved words, sentences, notes, tags, AI explanations, review schedule) — IndexedDB database named vocab, scoped to the extension.
  • Settings and your AI API key(s) — chrome.storage.local. The API key is stored locally so requests can go directly from your browser to the provider you configured; it is never sent to Vocab, because Vocab has no server.
  • Reading preferences — chrome.storage.local.
  • A random, locally generated identifier used to scope vocabulary data within the extension. It is never transmitted.
  • A short-lived translation cache — chrome.storage.session, cleared automatically when the browser restarts.

Nothing is stored on any Vocab server, because no Vocab server exists. Uninstalling the extension removes all of the above from your browser.

3. What is sent over the network

There are exactly two kinds of outbound request, both HTTPS:

a) Your configured AI provider

When you click an AI action (explain a word, generate examples, generate "Radar" related vocabulary, test a connection), the word or phrase you chose and its short sentence context are sent directly from your browser to the provider you configured — for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, or a local model such as Ollama or LM Studio running on your own machine. Vocab does not route, store or log these requests.

If you enable "auto-explain on save" (off by default), saving a word triggers one explanation request with that word and its sentence.

b) Google's keyless Translate endpoint

The bilingual-reading feature (on by default, can be turned off in Options) and quick translations send the text being read or translated to translate.googleapis.com — the same public, keyless endpoint the Google Translate widget uses. No API key is involved. Only extracted article prose (paragraphs, headings, sentences, and per-word glosses) of the page you are reading is sent — never the full page, scripts, forms or hidden content. Pages without article-like content are not sent.

What is never sent anywhere: your full vocabulary library, notes, tags, saved URLs, browsing history, page content when no reading or AI feature is active, and your API key (except to the provider it belongs to).

4. How your API key is handled

  • Vocab never supplies, generates or stores its own API key.
  • Your key is stored only in your browser's local extension storage.
  • Requests go directly from your browser to the provider you configured — Vocab has no backend that could see or proxy the key.
  • The key is sent only to the provider it belongs to, over HTTPS (or to a local model on localhost, which never leaves your machine).
  • The key is not included in backup/export files, is not written to logs, and appears masked in the settings UI.
  • You can remove a stored key at any time by editing or deleting the provider in Options.

Because the key is held in standard browser storage, anyone with access to your browser profile could in principle read it. We recommend scoping your key at the provider, setting a spending cap, and rotating it periodically.

5. Analytics, advertising and sharing

  • Analytics: none. Vocab contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting code.
  • Advertising: none.
  • Data sales: Vocab does not sell, rent or trade any data — it collects nothing to sell.
  • Sharing: the only third parties that ever see anything are (a) the AI provider you configured, for text you choose to have explained, and (b) Google's keyless Translate endpoint, for text you read with bilingual reading or quick-translate. Their respective terms and privacy policies apply to those requests.
  • Remote code: none. The extension is fully bundled; it loads no scripts from the internet.
  • Cookies: the extension sets no cookies.

6. Deleting your data

  • Open Options → Your data to clear all stored data, export a backup file (vocabulary only — never API keys), or import one.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes the IndexedDB database and extension storage from your browser.
  • Requests already sent to your AI provider or Google Translate cannot be recalled; see their privacy policies for their retention.

7. Changes to this policy

If the extension's data handling changes, this policy will be updated and the "last updated" date above will be revised. Vocab will never begin collecting data without updating this policy first.

Questions about this policy can be sent to the extension's maintainers. This extension is provided free of charge and without any warranty.
